\chapter{\uppercase{Research Gap and Problem Statement}}

\section{Research Gap}
Robo-advisory and AI financial planning literature covers general AI-driven advisory concepts, investor profiling, ethics, and Indian SIP adoption.

However, the reviewed literature does not integrate a dynamic, reward-shaped recommendation engine tied specifically to a goal's remaining time horizon.

The proposed project focuses on combining:
\begin{enumerate}
	\item Goal-based horizon-aware planning involving the target amount, deadline, and inflation-adjusted target.
	\item A mobile-first, India-relevant recommendation engine spanning mutual funds, equity, and debt instruments.
	\item Regret-based and future-looking Deep Reinforcement Learning reward concepts inspired by the base paper.
\end{enumerate}
The project therefore aims to bridge goal-based financial planning with regret-based and future-looking DRL reward shaping in a single mobile recommendation system.

\section{Problem Statement}
Individuals planning for financial goals such as retirement, a child's education, or purchasing a vehicle face difficulty determining how much to save, for how long, and where to invest.

The appropriate level of investment risk depends heavily on the goal's remaining time horizon.

There is a need for a system that dynamically adjusts investment recommendations based on both the time remaining to a goal and prevailing market conditions, rather than relying on fixed, one-time risk classifications.
