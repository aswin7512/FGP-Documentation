\chapter*{\centering \uppercase{Abstract}}%
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{\uppercase{Abstract}}

Individuals often find it difficult to plan investments for goals such as retirement, education, or vehicle purchase because the required savings, investment period, and acceptable risk vary with the goal timeline. Existing goal-based planning tools may use fixed risk categories and may not adapt recommendations to the time remaining for a goal. This project proposes a mobile application that provides personalized savings and investment planning across mutual funds, equity, and debt instruments based on a user's financial goal, time horizon, and risk profile. The recommendation engine is inspired by the base paper, ``Regret-Optimized Portfolio Enhancement through Deep Reinforcement Learning and Future Looking Rewards'', and adapts its regret-based and future-looking reward idea to goal-based, horizon-sensitive asset allocation.

The proposed system also includes inflation-adjusted goal calculation, SIP-related financial calculation, a Safety/Feasibility Calculator, risk profiling, dynamic allocation, and a mobile dashboard for tracking goal progress and recommended portfolio adjustments. Phase~1 establishes the literature foundation, research gap, problem statement, objectives, proposed architecture, and development plan for the system.
